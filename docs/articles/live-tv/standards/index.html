    Overview: Broadcast standards

    Digital television standards define signal, service and delivery conventions for particular broadcast systems.

    ATSC, DVB, ISDB-T and DTMB describe different broadcast families. Their definitions affect how a compatible tuner and receiver obtain programmes from the signal. This group gives each family its own context before the channel and guide information carried with it is interpreted.

    • Digital television standards
      Digital television standards define interoperable transmission, transport, signaling and media relationships whose supported combination determines a receiving and playback route.

    • ATSC family
      ATSC generations define distinct broadcast and service arrangements that require corresponding receiver support.

    • DVB family
      DVB delivery standards adapt digital television to terrestrial, cable or satellite transmission conditions.

    • ISDB-T
      ISDB-T defines segmented terrestrial transmission with layer/configuration relationships, while regional media profiles and the selected service determine later playback requirements.

    • DTMB
      DTMB is a terrestrial television transmission system defined by GB 20600, with its specific framing, coding and modulation required before service media can be recovered.
