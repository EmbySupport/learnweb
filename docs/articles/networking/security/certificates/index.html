    Overview: Certificate validation and lifecycle

    Certificate validation and lifecycle connect public identity evidence, trust paths, requested names and deployed certificate state.

    A certificate has to fit the requested service identity and lead through an acceptable validation path. Renewal also has to produce the certificate actually served to a new connection. These topics separate certificate contents, trust, name matching and deployment lifecycle so each observed failure can be located.

    • TLS certificates
      A TLS certificate binds public-key and service-identity information within a signed certificate structure whose usage, validity and trust must be evaluated by the client.

    • Certificate trust chains
      A certificate trust chain links endpoint evidence through valid issuer relationships to a client-trusted anchor, with path validation separate from service-name matching.

    • Certificate hostnames
      Certificate hostname validation compares the requested service identity with appropriately typed certificate names, keeping wildcard scope and direct IP identities distinct.

    • Certificate renewal and ACME
      Certificate renewal obtains and deploys successor TLS evidence through distinct validation, issuance, installation and endpoint-reload stages, which ACME can automate in part.
