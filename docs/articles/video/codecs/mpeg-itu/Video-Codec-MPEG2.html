    MPEG-2 Video

    MPEG-2 Video is the picture-compression format standardized as ITU-T H.262 and ISO/IEC 13818-2. It appears in many older disc transfers and television recordings.

    The name “MPEG-2” can also refer to the broader standards family. In a file's media information, distinguish the video codec from an MPEG systems container: a transport stream can carry codecs other than MPEG-2 Video.

    Name in a media discussion What it describes
    MPEG-2 Video / H.262 Compression of the moving pictures
    MPEG Program Stream A container combining streams
    MPEG Transport Stream A packet-based container
    MPEG Audio A separate family of audio coding formats

    MPEG-2 Video uses independently coded pictures and pictures predicted from others. Its groups of pictures affect random access and the amount of preceding video needed to reconstruct a chosen point. That explains why seeking is more involved than reading a single image.

    Preserve the recording's timing

    MPEG-2 Video can carry progressive or interlaced material. Converting a television recording is not just a decision about its codec: field timing, aspect ratio, and source cadence also matter. A careless conversion can discard motion detail or stretch the picture even when the new file is smaller.

    In Emby, an app with a suitable MPEG-2 decoder may play the source directly; another playback path may need video transcoding. Inspect the session's reason before assuming the format is damaged.

    Converting to H.264 or HEVC can make a derivative easier to deliver, but it cannot recreate detail the MPEG-2 source already discarded. The original recording remains the best reference when checking a conversion.
