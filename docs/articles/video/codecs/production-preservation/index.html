    Overview: Production and preservation codecs

    Production and preservation codecs serve workflows where editing access, reconstruction quality or long-term fidelity influence coding choices.

    A format chosen for editing may prioritize efficient picture access over the smallest delivery file. A preservation workflow can place another emphasis on exact reconstruction. The codecs in this group span those purposes; their individual pages distinguish picture access, compression and reconstruction properties.

    • Motion JPEG
      Motion JPEG stores video as independently JPEG-compressed pictures or fields, with packaging details that vary by implementation.

    • DV video
      DV video is an intra-frame compressed video format commonly encountered in digital tape transfers.

    • Apple ProRes
      Apple ProRes is a family of intra-frame video codecs used for production and editing, with separate variants for different quality and sample requirements.

    • Avid DNxHD
      Avid DNxHD identifies the established HD production-codec workflows within the VC-3 video coding family.

    • Avid DNxHR
      Avid DNxHR identifies resolution-independent production workflows in the VC-3 video coding family.

    • FFV1
      FFV1 is a lossless video coding format whose decoded sample values match the samples supplied to its encoder.
