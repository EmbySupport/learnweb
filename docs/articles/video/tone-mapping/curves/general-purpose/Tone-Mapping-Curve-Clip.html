    Tone Mapping Curve: Clip

    Clipping leaves in-range values alone and replaces every value above the output ceiling with that ceiling.

    Clipping puts a hard ceiling on a value. Below the ceiling it keeps the value unchanged; above it, every input becomes the same maximum.

    FFmpeg 8.1 clip mapping with scale parameter 1. Both axes use relative linear light for neutral gray; output white is 1.

    The flat section is the important part. Three parts of a bright cloud with inputs of 2, 4, and 8 all become 1. The cloud's positions remain, but the brightness differences that made its texture visible disappear.

    Input Output
    0.2 0.2
    1 1
    2 1
    8 1

    A highlight roll-off bends toward the limit gradually, leaving output space for some of those differences. It starts changing the values before they reach the ceiling. Clipping preserves the in-range part exactly and spends none of that range on brighter values.
