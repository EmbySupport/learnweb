    Chapter Markers

    A chapter marker is a bookmark on the media timeline. It can label the opening, a scene, or a song within a concert recording, letting the player jump to that position.

    Ordinary chapters do not divide the movie into separate video streams or require separate files. A chapter normally has a start time and may also have a title or an end time.

    Example marker What the player can offer
    00:00 — Opening A named starting point
    12:40 — The journey A jump into the existing timeline
    48:15 — Finale Another named destination

    These times are an illustrative chapter list, not prescribed intervals.

    Markers and previews are separate

    The text and timestamp of a chapter are different from a preview image. A thumbnail may be generated from the picture at a chapter position; adding chapter metadata alone does not embed that image.

    For an Emby file investigation, inspect the chapter list to establish whether named markers are actually present. A player can also provide navigation aids derived separately from the file.

    A chapter destination does not make the picture at that moment a keyframe. The player still uses the normal seeking and decoding process to reach it.

    Some formats support more elaborate chapter systems, including alternative editions or ordered segments. Those are additional features. A simple list of timestamps should be understood as bookmarks unless the file explicitly describes a different presentation.
