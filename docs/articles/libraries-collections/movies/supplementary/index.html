    Overview: Supplementary videos

    Supplementary movie videos attach supporting material such as extras and trailers to a feature.

    An interview or trailer can belong with a movie without being another version of its main programme. The association and presentation role determine how that video should appear in a library. These topics explain those roles so supplementary material stays recognizable beside the feature.

    • Movie extras
      Movie extras are supplementary resources whose role and relationship to a feature distinguish them from another complete movie version.

    • Movie trailers
      A movie trailer is a promotional video associated with a work or release, with its own edit, language, resource and playback role.
